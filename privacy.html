Privacy policy

This OAuth client is used only by its owner, to access the owner's own Google Drive with rclone running on the owner's own computer.

  • No data is collected, stored or processed by any server operated for this app.
  • Google Drive data is accessed only by the owner's local copy of rclone and stays between Google and the owner's computer.
  • No data is shared with, sold to or disclosed to any third party.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
